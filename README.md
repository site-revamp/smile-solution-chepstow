# Smile Solution Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Smile Solution is a UK orthodontic provider offering specialist treatment for children and adults through its practices in Bristol and Chepstow. Its services include fixed metal and ceramic braces, lingual braces, Invisalign and clear aligner treatment.

The redesigned homepage direction focuses on presenting Smile Solution as a specialist orthodontic provider, helping parents, teenagers and adults understand available treatment options and take the next step towards an appointment.

## 2. Main Improvements

1. **Clearer Specialist Orthodontic Positioning**  
   The homepage gives greater emphasis to Smile Solution's specialist orthodontic focus and professional background. This helps distinguish its core offering from general dental care.

2. **More Structured Treatment Information**  
   The treatment offering is organised around the main orthodontic options, including metal, ceramic and lingual braces, as well as Invisalign and clear aligners. This helps prospective patients identify relevant treatment choices more easily.

3. **Stronger Local Enquiry Focus**  
   The Bristol and Chepstow practice locations and their respective telephone numbers are made important parts of the homepage direction. This helps visitors identify their local practice and find the appropriate contact route.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Content hierarchy:** Gives specialist orthodontic care and treatment options greater prominence.
- **Treatment presentation:** Makes the different brace and aligner options easier to identify.
- **Local information:** Distinguishes the Bristol and Chepstow practices and their contact details.
- **Audience relevance:** Addresses the needs of parents, teenagers and adults considering orthodontic treatment.
- **Conversion focus:** Supports the primary objective of encouraging consultation and appointment enquiries.

## Project Objective

Create a clear, patient-focused homepage that communicates Smile Solution's specialist orthodontic services, presents its two practice locations and guides prospective patients towards making an appointment.
