A more personal approach

Orthodontics is about more than straight teeth.

Choosing orthodontic treatment is a personal decision. You want to understand your options, feel comfortable asking questions and know that your treatment is being considered by people with the right specialist experience.

Smile Solution focuses on specialist orthodontic care for adults and children, with treatment options that include Invisalign, ceramic fixed braces and lingual braces.

Every smile is different. A consultation is an opportunity to discuss your concerns, understand the options available and consider which approach may be suitable for you.

NHS specialist referrals

Looking for NHS orthodontic care?

The NHS listing states that the practice does not accept new NHS patients for routine dental care. It accepts new NHS patients for specialist dental care by clinical referral from another dentist.

Treatment availability, eligibility and referral arrangements can change. Contact Smile Solution or your referring dentist to confirm the current position.
